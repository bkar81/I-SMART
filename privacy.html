Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · IMART 1.0.0

Short version

IMART is designed as a local-first image annotation application. Normal annotation work does not require an IMART-operated application server. Images and Layers are processed in the browser.

What IMART stores locally

IMART stores tool settings, application preferences and related editor state in browser storage where needed. Settings can be exported to JSON for backup.

Your images and annotations

Images opened or pasted into IMART are processed by the browser for editing. IMART does not upload them to an IMART server as part of normal annotation. Exporting or sharing an image is an explicit action initiated by you.

Clipboard and browser APIs

Clipboard access and the system EyeDropper are optional browser capabilities. The browser may request permission, and availability depends on the browser and the context in which IMART is hosted.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

IMART does not require an application analytics or advertising system for its normal workflow. The standalone application does not require a server-side account.

Offline and PWA behavior

IMART can be installed as a Progressive Web App. Its service worker caches the application shell and supporting files for offline use. Browser capabilities that require network access naturally depend on the relevant browser or platform service.

Settings backups

When you export settings, the JSON file is created on your device and saved where you choose. You are responsible for protecting any exported backup that contains your configuration.

Third-party hosting

If IMART is hosted or forked by another party, that deployment may have its own hosting, analytics, or other services. Review the privacy information of the deployment you are using.

Your control

You control the images you open, the annotations you create, the files you export and the settings you back up. You can clear browser site data to remove locally stored application data.

Questions

Questions about IMART privacy behavior can be raised through the project's published contact or repository information.
